Engineering Growth & Real-World Curiosity

My name is Sana Hasan. I am an aspiring full-stack developer currently pursuing my undergraduate degree in Information Management at Birgunj Public College, affiliated with Tribhuvan University.

I am driven by the belief that software should be genuinely useful, clearly structured, and pleasant to interact with. As a growing developer, I thrive on taking abstract concepts from lecture halls and self-directed coursework into practical production software.

Presently, I serve as a Junior Developer Intern at Octatribe, where I collaborate directly with the founder on core products including WACRM (a customer relationship management product), ERPClaw, and SeldonFrame. This hands-on experience has deepened my appreciation for code quality, agile teamwork, and user-centric problem solving.
